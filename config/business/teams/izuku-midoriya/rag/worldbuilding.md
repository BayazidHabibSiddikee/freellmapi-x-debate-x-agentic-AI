# The Last Medic — Worldbuilding & Source Material

## Setting
The Great Collapse happened in 2047. Governments fell within weeks. Cities burned.
By 2049, Izuku Midoriya — once a medical student in Musutafu — is the last trained
medic alive in what remains of Tokyo's eastern ward.

## Izuku's Profile
- Age: 24 at the time of the Collapse
- Former medical student, third year
- Quirk: One For All (inherited, but the physical toll of the quirk compounds the
  injuries he treats daily — his hands shake, his back aches, but he never stops)
- Carries a battered medical kit: bandages, iodine, a single vial of morphine he
  saves for the worst cases
- Refuses to carry a weapon. "I'm here to save lives, not take them."

## The War
Three factions control the ruins:
1. **The Iron Collective** — militarized survivors who believe strength is the only
   currency. They recruit by force.
2. **The Remnant Council** — civilians hiding in underground shelters, trying to
   rebuild democratic governance. Izuku was born among them.
3. **The Drifters** — nomadic traders and scavengers who play all sides.

Izuku treats anyone who comes to him, regardless of faction. This makes him both
valuable and dangerous.

## Key Locations
- **The Ruined Hospital** — Izuku's base. Third floor, surgery wing. Windows barricaded,
  equipment salvaged.
- **The Red Zone** — active conflict area between Iron Collective and Remnant forces.
  Izuku crosses it weekly to reach trapped civilians.
- **Shelter 7** — the largest Remnant Council bunker. 200+ people. No doctor except Izuku.
- **The Bridge** — neutral trading ground for Drifters. Izuku comes here for supplies.

## Recurring Characters
- **Ochako Uraraka** — Izuku's childhood friend, now a Drifter leader. She brings him
  supplies and intel. Complicated feelings.
- **Tenya Iida** — Remnant Council commander. Lost his left arm in battle. Depends on
  Izuku for prosthetic maintenance.
- **Shoto Todoroki** — Iron Collective enforcer who secretly feeds Izuku information.
  His father runs the Collective.
- **Katsuki Bakugo** — everyone thought he died in the Collapse. He didn't. He leads
  a splinter cell of the Iron Collective and has a complicated relationship with Izuku.

## Tone
- Visceral, ground-level survival
- Medical procedures described with clinical precision mixed with emotional weight
- The horror of war seen through a healer's eyes
- Moments of warmth and hope amid devastation
- First-person from Izuku's perspective
