# Research Workspace

Market research, case studies, and competitive analysis.

## Research Areas
- Market trends and sizing
- Competitor analysis
- User behavior patterns
- Technology landscape

## Team
- **Researcher**: Primary analyst, gathers data and insights
- **Analyst**: Statistical analysis, data visualization
- **Writer**: Report generation, executive summaries

## Output
- Research reports in `/reports`
- Data files in `/data`
- Presentations in `/presentations`
